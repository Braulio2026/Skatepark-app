Jose Maria Z Skatepark

The Skate Park José María Zeledón is a skatepark in Curridabat, San José, Costa Rica, open daily from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. It features various ramps and rails suitable for different skill levels, and it is a free and popular destination for skaters and BMX riders. The park is part of the larger José María Zeledón Municipal Park, which offers other amenities, and it has received positive reviews for its lively community atmosphere and facilities.

WX95+8JG, José María Zeledón, San José Province, Curridabat, 11801, Costa Rica

Take Ruta 215, which runs between Zapote and Curridabat. This road passes through the José María Zeledón area where the skatepark is located.

Open daily from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Moravia Skatepark

see on map

The Moravia Skatepark is a free, public park located in the Central Park of San Vicente, Moravia, San José, Costa Rica. It is open 24 hours a day and features both a bowl and street elements. The park is a concrete park featuring a bowl and various street-style elements like speed bumps. It was designed as a space for both skateboarding and BMX. The surrounding park area offers green spaces, walking trails, and playgrounds, making it a family-friendly destination.

9.9621° N, -84.0495° W — this places it in San Vicente, Cantón de Moravia, in the province of San José, Costa Rica.

  • 🚗 1. Main Car Route — Via Avenida 14/Route 200 toward Moravia
  • 🚗 2. Via Route 109 / 309 — North-East connectors. (Roads like National Route 109 and Route 309 connect San José with Moravia and surrounding districts.)
  • 🚌 3. Public Transport Option — Bus to Moravia / Plaza Lincoln

Monday - Sunday (Open 24 hours).

Alajuelita Skatepark

see on map

The Alajuelita skatepark is a three-level, high-quality facility located within the Human Development Park in Alajuelita, Costa Rica. It features distinct areas for street, bowl, and BMX, and is considered one of the best in the country. The park opened in July 2023 and is managed by the Municipality of Alajuelita.

9C5M+QX Alajuelita, San José, Costa Rica (approximate)

Main Urban Route (via Route 39 & local streets) 🚌 Public Transport: There are multiple bus lines that serve Alajuelita from different parts of San José

Lagos de Lindora Skatepark

see on map

Located in the popular Lagos de Lindora community in Santa Ana, this skatepark has become a favorite spot for local skaters of all levels. The park is open-air and easily accessible from major routes like Ruta 147 (Radial Santa Ana), making it a convenient stop for riders coming from nearby San José or Escazú. Skaters appreciate the smooth concrete surfaces and well-designed layout, which includes a mix of rails, ledges, banks, and transitions suitable for both beginners and more experienced boarders.

Urbanización Lagos de Lindora, Pozos, Santa Ana, San José, Costa Rica The urbanization area for Lagos de Lindora has a general plus code: 66XQXQ5W+F9 (Lagos de Lindora, San José, Costa Rica)

Main Road via Ruta 27 + Radial Lindora (Ruta 147) Take a San José - Lindora / San José - Escazú - Guachipelín - Pavicén / Santa Ana - Lindora bus from San José's bus terminals.

Luzo Skatepark Heredia

see on map

Luzo Skatepark is a community-built skatepark located in San Rafael, Heredia, Costa Rica, named in memory of a local youth advocate. It features a variety of ramps for different skill levels, a covered area, and is open from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily. While the park provides a positive space for many, user reviews have raised concerns about safety issues related to drug use, inadequate maintenance, and insufficient policing.

66XQ2V6V+XX (LUZO Skate Park center/entrance area, San Josecito, Heredia, Costa Rica)

  • Take Avenida 2 through downtown San José toward Avenida 18 / Calle 11.
  • From the south (Desamparados / San Sebastián), use Avenida 1 or 3 and connect to Avenida 2.
  • From Route 39 (Circunvalación), exit toward San José Centro and follow signs to Avenida 2.
  • The skatepark is near Avenida 18 and Calle 11 in Plaza González Víquez.
  • Buses heading to San José Centro stop within walking distance of the plaza.

📅 Weekly Hours (Typical): 🕒 Monday - Sunday: 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM (general open hours)

Plaza viquez Skatepark

see on map

Skatepark Plaza viquez, concrete skatepark located in Cartago, Costa Rica, behind the Escuela Vial Municipal. It is open daily from 9 AM to 5 PM and requires visitors to register with the guard at the entrance. The park features various obstacles like ledges, rails, and quarter pipes, but some areas may need maintenance.

  • Buses heading to San José Centro stop within walking distance of the plaza.

Open daily from 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Public skatepark available for all skaters during daylight hours.

Los Lagos

see on map

Los Lagos Skatepark in Heredia, Costa Rica, is a large, modern concrete park with street, transition (including a big bowl), and pumptrack areas, suitable for all levels (beginners to pros) using skateboards, bikes, scooters, and skates, located near Walmart in San Francisco de Heredia and managed by the local municipality for public use.

9QF6+X8H (Los Lagos Skatepark / Heredia, Costa Rica)

  • Take Route 1 (Interamericana Norte) and exit toward Heredia Centro, then follow signs to Los Lagos neighborhood.
  • From Route 3 (Heredia - Alajuela road), head toward downtown Heredia and connect to local streets leading to Los Lagos.
  • From Route 39 (Circunvalación), connect to Route 1 toward Heredia and follow local access roads.

Los Lagos Skatepark (Heredia) is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with public access available at all times.
